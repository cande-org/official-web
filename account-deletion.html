Request deletion without the app

Email an account deletion request

Contact: contact@cande.fyi

Use the subject “Jeongbyeong Balsa account deletion request” and provide your sign-in email or the account identifier shown in the app. We confirm the account and carry out proportionate ownership verification before processing the request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents or card numbers.

Delete directly in the app

Open My Page → Delete Account, review the account and credit notice, then confirm deletion. Uninstalling or signing out does not delete the account.

Data and subscriptions

Deletion covers the service account and its profile, AI friends, rooms, chat history, credit data and private uploaded files. Deleted account records and remaining credits cannot be recovered.

Separate rules apply to legally required transaction or dispute records and external processors’ logs or backups. If retention is required, we explain the reason and applicable period when processing the request. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel in the store where you subscribed to stop further renewals.
